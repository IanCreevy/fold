Fold

Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Fold is a paper-folding puzzle. You can finish every level without paying. The game is ad-free. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Who to contact

Questions about privacy: contact-us@navigatorofshame.com

Information stored on your device

Fold keeps game progress on the device itself:

  • Levels you have unlocked and stars you have earned
  • The traveler skin you have selected
  • Skins the store says you own, once billing is connected

That information stays on the device. Fold does not ask you to create an account.

Purchases

Optional skins are sold by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Fold does not receive your card number. Apple or Google handles payment, receipts, and taxes. Use Restore purchases in the skin shop to bring back skins already bought with that store account.

Ads and tracking

Fold does not show ads. Fold does not use advertising identifiers, and it does not sell personal information.

Children

Fold is a general-audience puzzle. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not ask for a name, email, or precise location.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change. The current version is always at this page.
